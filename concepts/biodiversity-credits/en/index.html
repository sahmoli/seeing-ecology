Biodiversity credits

生物多样性信用

A biodiversity credit is a certificate representing a measured, evidence-based, durable, and additional unit of positive biodiversity outcome — an outcome accounting unit rather than a permit to destroy nature elsewhere, focused here on voluntary non-offset nature contributions.

“…a certificate that represents a measured and evidence-based unit of positive biodiversity outcome that is durable and additional to what would have otherwise occurred.”

BCA 2024 · Wunder & Fraccaroli 2025 · Wauchope et al. 2024

Illustrative 100 ha scheme arithmetic (1 gross unit per ha per 1% relative uplift); arithmetic outputs require third-party verification and public registry issuance (Wauchope et al. 2024; Wunder et al. 2025).

Criteria

High-Integrity Requirements

  • Additionality: measured ecological gains exceed a credible without-project counterfactual baseline; high static condition alone is not uplift.
  • High-integrity multi-metric basket: tracks habitat structure alongside multiple native taxonomic groups (some early schemes still use composite indices, though single-species tree cover cannot represent community integrity).
  • Durability, audit & governance: long-term permanence (typically ≥ 20 years), leakage and buffer deductions, third-party registry retirement, and IPLC rights safeguards.

Common, Not Required

  • Bundled with carbon credits (biodiversity is spatially non-fungible and cannot copy 1 tCO₂e pricing)
  • Used to offset buyer damage (IAPB 2024 rejects international offsetting, favouring voluntary nature contributions)
  • Single global unit of nature (metrics must anchor to local reference ecosystems)

Distinctions

Voluntary credit自愿生物多样性信用
Nature contributionAudited uplift
Certifies and funds verified, durable, additional biodiversity gains without compensating damage caused elsewhere by the buyer — the primary high-integrity pathway endorsed by Wauchope et al. (2024) and IAPB (2024).
Biodiversity offset生物多样性抵消
Damage compensationNo Net Loss goal
Used at the end of the mitigation hierarchy to compensate residual development impacts; credits are an accounting unit whereas offsetting is a claim use that risks becoming a licence to trash if traded across ecosystems (Maron et al. 2012).
Carbon credit碳信用
1 tCO₂e fungibleWell-mixed gas
Atmospheric CO₂ mixes globally so 1 tonne abated anywhere has equivalent climate physics; biodiversity is governed by endemism, food webs, and spatial configuration, making units fundamentally non-fungible across biomes (Wunder et al. 2025).
Single-proxy claim单一代理指标凭证
Monoculture / stockingGreenwashing risk
Certificates issued solely on fast-growing tree canopy cover or single-species stocking counts can coincide with declining native understorey and specialist fauna, violating ecosystem-integrity standards.

Colombia Terrasos (Tebu)20-year habitat bank & milestone release

In Andean cloud forests (spectacled bear habitat), each voluntary Tebu unit represents 10 m² of conserved or restored ecosystem under a minimum 20-year commitment, released in audited tranches and retired on a public registry.

Metrics

2024 Wauchope et al. Counterfactual BACI
Counterfactual Additionality ΔB(t) & Multi-Metric State

Plain intuition: Creditable gain is not how rich a site looks today, but how much extra biodiversity exists with the project minus what would have happened anyway without it.

ΔB(t) = Bproj(t) − Bcf(t), B = ∑m wm · fm(Im)∑m wm
Bproj(t) Composite biodiversity condition under project intervention, normalized across multiple field indicators Im relative to a local reference ecosystem (fm ∈ [0, 1]). Bcf(t) Credible without-project counterfactual trajectory estimated from matched control sites, BACI designs, or verified regional decline rates.
Wauchope et al. 2024, Proc R Soc B 291:20242353
  • Aggregation compensation: Means, weighted sums, and medians all allow declines in sensitive specialists to be masked by gains elsewhere unless disaggregated floors are enforced.
  • Unobservable counterfactual: Because the without-project world is never directly observed on the same plot, estimation uncertainty must be discounted conservatively.
  • Claim discipline: Verified uplift supports a positive contribution claim, not equivalence to biodiversity damaged elsewhere by the buyer.

Debates

Market Liquidity vs. Non-Fungibility

Financial markets prefer standardized, liquid units like 1 tCO₂e, yet a 1% gain in savanna is ecologically non-interchangeable with a 1% gain in Andean cloud forest. Over-standardizing composite percentages risks obscuring irreplaceable local endemism.

Wauchope et al. 2024 · Wunder et al. 2025

Restoration Uplift vs. Intact Stewardship

Degraded pastures readily generate steep upward metric curves, whereas intact Indigenous-stewarded rainforests remain near equilibrium (ΔB ≈ 0). Rewarding only counterfactual uplift starves intact ecosystems unless distinct stewardship certificates are recognized.

Wauchope et al. 2024 · Wunder et al. 2025

Voluntary Demand vs. Offset Linkage

Because purely philanthropic corporate demand remains thin, some developers advocate linking credits to regulatory compliance offsets; conservation scientists warn cross-biome or cross-taxa trading would turn credits into licences for local habitat loss.

zu Ermgassen et al. 2019 · Wunder et al. 2025

Commodifying Noise & IPLC Revenue Share

Short verification intervals risk mistaking natural population fluctuations and sampling error for project uplift (“commodifying noise”); meanwhile high eDNA, acoustic, and third-party audit overheads can squeeze the share reaching Indigenous Peoples and Local Communities.

Wauchope et al. 2024 · Peng et al. 2024

Links

Habitat fragmentation→
Equal restored area yields very different outcomes depending on patch size, edge exposure, and connectivity — explaining why biodiversity credits are spatially non-fungible unlike carbon tons (Wauchope et al. 2024).
Umbrella species→
Relying on a single umbrella species' habitat as a credit proxy risks missing declines in non-target specialists and cannot replace multi-taxa basket verification (Carlisle et al. 2018).
Keystone species→
Restoring keystones can drive nonlinear recovery, yet credit aggregation rules must prevent gains in common generalists from masking local extirpation of strong interactors (Wauchope et al. 2024).
Flagship species→
Flagship branding helps mobilize voluntary corporate finance, but conflating marketing salience with audited counterfactual uplift invites biodiversity greenwashing (Wunder et al. 2025).
